# D-99: Known limitations of version 1

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Area:** Scope
- **From:** Version 1 (Phases 1–12)
- **Recorded:** 2026-10-24

## Context

A decision in the **Scope** area, recorded during Version 1 (Phases 1–12). It affects these parts of the specification: README, D.5.

## Decision

**Known limitations of version 1**: one Mac, SQLite and plain HTTP (not for internet exposure); no notifications or email, so no "forgot password" — HR resets passwords (D-91); no holiday calendar or delegated approvals; one company time zone for every deadline; payroll CSV in the app's own format; a changed expected weekly hours value has no per-week history (D-87). See D.5 for what comes next.
